Frequently Asked Questions

Direct engineering guidance regarding PCB layer stackups, microcontroller architectures, firmware capabilities, and industrial protocol support.

1. Does SKJAL provide custom PCB design services in India?

Yes. Operating from Innovation Valley in Visakhapatnam, SKJAL provides complete PCB design and hardware engineering services across India. Deliverables include schematic capture, 2-to-16 layer multilayer layouts, impedance matching, micro-via routing, thermal dissipation management, signal integrity simulation, BOM optimization, and rapid functional prototyping.

2. Can SKJAL develop embedded firmware for STM32, ESP32, and ARM Cortex?

Yes. Our firmware engineers build bare-metal C/C++, FreeRTOS, and Zephyr RTOS firmware for ARM Cortex-M0/M3/M4/M7, STM32, ESP32, Nordic nRF52, and RISC-V architectures. We develop custom peripheral drivers (SPI, I2C, UART), low-power sleep management (<10μA sleep currents), secure boot, hardware AES encryption, and fail-safe dual-bank Firmware-Over-The-Air (FOTA) update systems.

3. Can you develop custom industrial IoT gateways for specific field protocols?

Yes. We engineer turnkey DIN-rail and ruggedized IP67 edge gateways tailored to your machinery interfaces. Supported physical layers and buses include isolated RS-485, RS-232, CAN 2.0B / CAN-FD, and Ethernet, bridging field devices to cloud SCADA via 4G LTE, NB-IoT, or LoRaWAN.

4. Which industrial communication protocols are supported?

We support Modbus RTU, Modbus ASCII, Modbus TCP, CANopen, J1939, BACnet, OPC-UA, MQTT, HTTP/HTTPS, and proprietary serial packet protocols, ensuring interoperability with legacy PLCs, drives, and modern cloud telemetry platforms.

5. What information is required to request an engineering quotation?

To request an engineering quote, provide a high-level system overview, target input/output interfaces, power source constraints, desired communication channels (4G, LoRa, Ethernet), anticipated operating environment, target prototype timeline, and projected production volume. Technical feasibility and initial BOM reviews are delivered within 24 to 48 business hours.
