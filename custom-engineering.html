Turnkey engineering process.

From initial schematic capture to mass surface mount assembly: how SKJAL engineers hardware from concept to deployment.

STEP 01

Schematic & Architecture

Component selection, power budget modeling, and schematic design optimized for domestic Indian supply chain availability and lowest BOM cost.

STEP 02

Multilayer PCB Layout

Signal and power integrity analysis, impedance-controlled routing, EMC/EMI suppression filtering, and 3D enclosure collision modeling.

STEP 03

RTOS Firmware Sprints

Bare-metal driver implementation, FreeRTOS/Zephyr task design, edge encryption, and automated hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing.

STEP 04

DFM & Mass Production

Design for Manufacturing (DFM) verification, automated pick-and-place SMT assembly, optical inspection, and turnkey enclosure integration.
