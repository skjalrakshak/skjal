End-to-End Capabilities

Full-Lifecycle PCB Design Company in Vizag

High-performance electronics demand meticulous signal integrity (SI), power distribution network (PDN) optimization, and thermal dissipation modeling. Operating out of our advanced hardware laboratory in Innovation Valley, Visakhapatnam, our engineering team designs 2-layer to 16-layer printed circuit boards tailored for industrial sensors, automotive telemetry, and harsh-environment edge controllers.

💿

High-Speed Multilayer PCB Layout

Impedance-matched differential routing for USB 3.0, Ethernet (10/100/1000 Mbps), MIPI, and PCIe. Comprehensive blind, buried, and micro-via architectures minimizing board surface area while maintaining strict CE, FCC, and RoHS compliance.

⚙️

Custom Embedded Firmware Development

Production-grade C/C++ firmware built for ARM Cortex-M0+/M4/M7, ESP32-S3, STM32, Nordic nRF52, and RISC-V architectures. We implement robust multitasking using FreeRTOS and Zephyr RTOS with secure bootloaders and Fail-Safe Dual-Bank Over-The-Air (FOTA) update mechanisms.

📡

Turnkey Industrial IIoT Edge Gateways

Ruggedized DIN-rail and IP67 gateways integrating RS485, Modbus RTU/TCP, CAN bus, OPC-UA, 4G LTE Cat-1/Cat-M, NB-IoT, and LoRaWAN. Direct edge-to-cloud payload encryption via TLS 1.3 and hardware cryptographic security chips (ATECC608B).

🏭

Low-Cost Custom IoT Hardware Manufacturing (DFM/DFA)

Design for Manufacturing (DFM) and Design for Assembly (DFA) audits eliminating supply-chain bottlenecks. We leverage high-volume indigenous surface mount assembly (SMT) partnerships in India, lowering total cost of ownership (TCO) by 40% compared to overseas OEM imports.
