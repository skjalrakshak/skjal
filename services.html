How we build mission-critical hardware.

From initial hardware specifications to volume production, our structured 5-stage sprint model ensures zero design oversights.

01. Technical Scoping

Comprehensive review of environmental factors, operating temperatures, sensor electrical interfaces, and target BOM budget.

02. Schematic & Routing

High-precision schematic capture, multilayer PCB trace simulation, component tolerance stress analysis, and prototype fab.

03. Firmware Bring-Up

Bare-metal and RTOS driver bring-up, peripheral handshake verification, low-power sleep profiling, and fail-safe coding.

04. Lab & Field Pilot

Rigorous environmental cycling, EMI pre-compliance bench checks at KLTIF Incubator, and live field telemetry validation.
