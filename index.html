(01) About

We started with a simple question: why does so much water disappear before it ever reaches people?

SK Jalrakshak Innovations (CIN: U26517AP2025PTC119413) was born from frustration with the status quo. Across India, aging pipelines leak billions of gallons daily while operators rely on spreadsheets and guesswork. We knew there had to be a better way.

So we built it. Our edge sensors go where no monitoring system has gone before — deep underground, in corrosive treatment tanks, across hundreds of miles of rural pipeline. They talk to our cloud in real-time, and our AI spots trouble before humans ever could. The result? Operators who sleep better at night, communities with cleaner water, and infrastructure that actually works the way it should.
