(01) About

We started with a simple question: why does so much water disappear before it ever reaches people?

SK Jalrakshak Innovations (CIN: U26517AP2025PTC119413) was founded to solve a problem hiding in plain sight: India loses over 40% of its treated water to infrastructure failures that nobody sees coming. Aging pipelines rupture underground, chemical levels drift unnoticed, and operators are left managing billion-dollar networks with spreadsheets and manual readings.

We engineered the solution from first principles. Our ruggedized edge sensors deploy directly onto existing pipes, tanks, and treatment plants — streaming 2.4 million data points per minute to our cloud intelligence platform. Machine learning models trained on millions of real-world events detect anomalies 72 hours before they become emergencies, turning reactive crisis management into proactive infrastructure stewardship.

What is SK Jalrakshak Innovations?

Registered under CIN: U26517AP2025PTC119413, we serve utilities and operators across India.

How do I get in touch?

Reach our engineering team directly at info.skjipl@gmail.com. We respond to all enquiries within 24 hours with a preliminary assessment. For site visits, our team operates from Visakhapatnam with deployment capability across all Indian states.

What is your full service offering?

We provide end-to-end water infrastructure intelligence: IP68-rated edge telemetry hardware, a unified cloud monitoring platform with real-time geo-mapping, ML-powered predictive analytics engines, solar-powered off-grid IoT nodes with satellite backhaul, automated compliance reporting (CPCB, WHO, ISO 24512), and custom SCADA/API integrations.
