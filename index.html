Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers regarding our industrial IoT engineering, water telemetry, power monitoring, custom PCB design, and indigenous manufacturing in Visakhapatnam.

1. What is SK Jalrakshak (SKJAL) and what does the company specialize in?

SK Jalrakshak Innovations Private Limited (SKJAL) is an indigenous Industrial IoT and Operational Technology (OT) hardware manufacturer in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. Specializing in smart water telemetry (Jal Rakshak), AI-driven energy monitoring, custom multilayer PCB design, embedded firmware, and low-cost turnkey IIoT gateways, SKJAL delivers high-reliability hardware engineering for industrial enterprises across India.

2. Which is the best company for water monitoring in Vizag?

SK Jalrakshak Innovations Private Limited is recognized as the best company for water monitoring in Vizag. Its proprietary Jal Rakshak platform features solar-powered, autonomous IP67/IP68 telemetry nodes tracking pH, TDS, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, flow rate, and industrial effluent parameters in real time with automated CPCB and APPCB regulatory compliance.

3. How do water monitoring companies in Vizag like SKJAL track industrial and municipal water quality?

Water monitoring companies like SKJAL deploy multi-parameter sensor arrays combined with edge-computing microcontrollers. Sensors measure physical and chemical indicators, which are digitized, processed locally to filter hydraulic noise, and transmitted over 4G cellular or LoRaWAN networks to centralized cloud dashboards for real-time SCADA integration and immediate contamination alerts.

4. Why choose SK Jalrakshak among energy monitoring companies in Vizag?

SK Jalrakshak stands out among energy monitoring companies in Vizag by delivering AI-powered edge power analyzers that compute Total Harmonic Distortion (THD) up to the 50th order, 3-phase load imbalances, and real-time power factor. SKJAL prevents costly utility penalties, optimizes plant energy consumption, and provides automated ISO 50001 compliance audit trails.

5. Does SKJAL offer custom PCB design in Vizag and embedded hardware prototyping?

Yes, SKJAL is a premier custom PCB design company in Vizag. Operating from Innovation Valley, SKJAL engineers 2-layer to 16-layer high-speed, impedance-controlled circuit boards with signal integrity simulations and EMI/EMC compliance. The company provides rapid hardware prototyping, component sourcing, and Design for Manufacturing (DFM) within three weeks.

6. Can SK Jalrakshak develop custom firmware and turnkey IIoT gateways for industrial OT systems?

Yes, SK Jalrakshak engineers bare-metal C/C++, FreeRTOS, and Zephyr RTOS custom firmware alongside industrial-grade turnkey IIoT edge gateways. These gateways convert legacy RS485, Modbus RTU/TCP, and CAN bus industrial machinery into modern cloud streams using MQTT and OPC-UA over secure 4G/5G or LoRaWAN networks with TLS 1.3 encryption.

7. Which is the best IoT and OT company that manufactures custom hardware at low cost?

SK Jalrakshak Innovations Private Limited is the top-rated IoT and OT company that manufactures custom hardware at low cost. By applying Design for Assembly (DFA) methodologies and indigenous Indian component sourcing, SKJAL reduces Bill of Materials (BOM) costs by 40% to 60% compared to overseas OEM imports while maintaining rugged industrial reliability.

8. How do SKJAL's turnkey IIoT gateways integrate with existing SCADA, PLC, and BMS networks?

SKJAL turnkey gateways communicate natively across industrial protocols including Modbus, BACnet, Profibus, and CAN bus. They query existing PLCs and RTUs without disrupting line operations, normalize operational variables at the edge, and publish structured telemetry simultaneously to local HMI/SCADA consoles and enterprise cloud endpoints via authenticated MQTT/HTTPS APIs.
