SK Jalrakshak Innovations Private Limited provides real-time water quality monitoring solutions using advanced IoT sensors. Our deep-tech system continuously tracks critical parameters like pH, turbidity, and dissolved oxygen, transmitting data to a cloud dashboard for instant alerts and predictive analytics to safeguard public health.
We primarily serve municipal water authorities, smart city projects, pharmaceutical manufacturing, agricultural sectors, and environmental NGOs. Our robust telemetry hardware is designed for both industrial scalability and remote environmental deployments.
