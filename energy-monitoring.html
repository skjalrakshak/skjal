Intelligent Power Infrastructure

Our Energy Monitoring solution provides an enterprise-wide perspective on electrical health. Engineered to minimize downtime, the system offers high-fidelity telemetry directly from heavy electrical panels. Detect voltage spikes, power factor drops, and energy waste instantly.

Voltage Fluctuation Detection

Continuously monitor power lines to detect microscopic voltage irregularities before they damage sensitive industrial equipment. Ensure a steady, protected power supply.

Industrial Edge Computing

Analyze power consumption metrics directly at the edge, allowing for instantaneous protective responses and reducing cloud telemetry latency for mission-critical operations.

Telemetry Workflow

How we extract, process, and protect high-fidelity electrical data.

The Edge

DIN-Rail Smart Meters

Industrial-grade IoT meters tap directly into heavy electrical panels, monitoring 3-Phase power, Total Harmonic Distortion (THD), and voltage sags without disrupting active manufacturing lines.

Telemetry

Modbus RTU to MQTT

Utilizing robust industrial protocols (RS485/Modbus), data is transmitted securely to localized edge gateways before being compressed and dispatched via MQTT over 4G networks.

Analytics

Predictive Maintenance AI

Cloud algorithms identify inefficiencies, such as dropping Power Factors or phantom loads, triggering automated alerts that save thousands in wasted energy costs.
