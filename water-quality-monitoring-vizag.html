Autonomous Water Quality Monitoring in Visakhapatnam

SK Jalrakshak Innovations manufactures the only autonomous water quality monitoring grid engineered specifically for Visakhapatnam's industrial and municipal sectors. Our IoT sensors eliminate manual grab sampling, transmitting real-time compliance data directly from effluent discharge points and municipal reservoirs.

Real-Time Compliance Tracking

Industrial facilities in Andhra Pradesh face strict environmental compliance regulations. Our autonomous water quality sensors continuously measure pH, turbidity, Total Dissolved Solids (TDS), and flow rates. This data streams to our telemetry dashboard, generating automated logs for environmental audits.

Robust Sensor Node Engineering

Water monitoring hardware requires extreme durability. Our sensor nodes are manufactured in Visakhapatnam using corrosion-resistant probes and self-cleaning mechanisms, drastically reducing maintenance cycles in high-particulate municipal water lines and aquaculture operations.

Eliminating Manual Latency

Manual water testing introduces dangerous latency between contamination events and detection. Our autonomous water quality monitoring grid operates 24/7. If a toxic spike occurs, the edge computing nodes instantly trigger SMS and email alerts to facility managers, preventing major environmental incidents.

Municipal and Government Integration

We actively deploy water quality telemetry systems for government infrastructure projects across Andhra Pradesh. By providing a scalable, localized hardware ecosystem, we ensure municipal water supplies maintain strict quality standards via continuous, automated monitoring.
