Automated Data Transmission

Our telemetry hardware eliminates the need for manual site audits. By deploying our IoT nodes onto legacy equipment, industrial operators receive continuous data streams regarding power consumption, vibration, and temperature without deploying field technicians.

Secure Industrial Protocols

Data security is paramount in heavy industry. Our telemetry solutions utilize encrypted MQTT protocols and cellular IoT networks (NB-IoT/LTE-M) to ensure machine data is transmitted securely to our cloud dashboards, entirely bypassing vulnerable local Wi-Fi networks.

Edge Computing Integration

Rather than flooding the cloud with raw data, our Visakhapatnam-manufactured telemetry nodes feature edge computing capabilities. The hardware analyzes voltage drops and flow rates locally, instantly transmitting high-priority alerts when parameters breach compliance thresholds.

Custom Dashboards for Andhra Pradesh Industry

All telemetry data feeds into our proprietary software dashboards. Whether you are managing an aquaculture farm, a steel manufacturing plant, or a municipal water grid, our interface provides real-time visualization of your distributed assets.
