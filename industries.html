Engineering Verification

Tested for Indian Field Conditions

Every enclosure, printed circuit board, and firmware stack is engineered to withstand tropical monsoon rains, high-humidity coastal heat, and severe electrical surges.

IP67 / IP68 Ingress Protection

Sealed cast aluminum and UV-stabilized polycarbonate enclosures with watertight cable glands and Gore vents.

Surge & Transient Immunity

Isolated RS485 transceivers, TVS diodes, and galvanic protection against substation switching transients.

Cellular Failover Intelligence

Onboard non-volatile circular flash buffer storing up to 60 days of sensor data during network outages.

Indigenous Hardware Supply

100% locally sourced components and manufactured pan-India under the national Make in India initiative.
