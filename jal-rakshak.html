End-to-end telemetry architecture.

From submerged physical sensors to actionable decision dashboards—how Jal Rakshak operates in the field.

STEP 01

Submerged Probes

Industrial-grade anti-corrosive probes continuously sample physical and chemical metrics directly in the water body.

STEP 02

Edge Processing

On-board microcontroller digitizes raw analog signals, applies temperature calibration curves, and checks local alarm limits.

STEP 03

Secure Telemetry

Data packets are encrypted via TLS and transmitted over cellular or mesh network to the central cloud ingestion endpoint.

STEP 04

Decision Dashboard

Plant operators and municipal teams view live status, receive instantaneous anomaly alarms, and review trend forecasts.
