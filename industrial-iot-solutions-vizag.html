Rugged Hardware for Harsh Environments

Industrial IoT requires hardware that survives. We engineer our sensor enclosures with IP65/IP67 ratings to withstand the particulate dust, extreme temperatures, and moisture commonly found in steel plants and pharma labs. By manufacturing locally in Visakhapatnam, we guarantee rapid hardware replacement and immediate engineering support.

Custom Telemetry Networks

Our industrial IoT solutions rely on secure telemetry networks that bypass vulnerable factory Wi-Fi systems. We utilize cellular IoT (NB-IoT/LTE-M) to transmit machine-level data directly to encrypted cloud dashboards, isolating critical operational technology from corporate IT vulnerabilities.

Predictive Maintenance Integration

By deploying our industrial IoT sensors onto legacy manufacturing equipment, operators gain real-time visibility into machine health. Our edge-computing microcontrollers process vibration and temperature data locally, triggering instant alerts when assets deviate from their baseline signatures.
