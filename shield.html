S.H.I.E.L.D. monitors in-cabin ergonomics, continuous whole-body vibration (WBV) exposure, driver alertness, and speed context to prevent road incidents and protect driver health.

Protection grounded in biometric & kinematic physics.

Traditional telematics tracks only GPS coordinates. S.H.I.E.L.D. tracks the driver's physical well-being, road shocks, and cognitive alertness in real time.

Whole-Body Vibration (WBV)

Tri-axial MEMS accelerometers calibrated to ISO 2631-1 calculate Cumulative Daily Exposure A(8) to safeguard long-haul drivers against spinal trauma.

  • StandardISO 2631-1 & ISO 5349 Compliant
  • Measurement AxisTri-Axial (X, Y, Z) Dynamic
  • Frequency Range0.5 Hz – 100 Hz Filtered
  • Threshold AlarmsAction Value (EAV) Warnings
  • Seat SensorThin Piezo-Resistive Mat

In-cabin edge intelligence workflow.

Low-latency edge processing ensures drivers receive instant audible coaching before an accident occurs, not hours later in a back-office report.

STEP 01

Seat & Cabin Sensing

Vibration sensors on the seat base and cabin sensors stream high-frequency kinematic and ambient data to the edge unit.

STEP 02

Edge Ergonomic Engine

The on-board processor computes vibration dose values (VDV) and fatigue risk indexes locally in real time.

STEP 03

Immediate Driver Audio

If fatigue or cumulative vibration thresholds are breached, the unit delivers clear audible prompts advising a rest break.

STEP 04

Fleet Analytics Cloud

Aggregated risk profiles, route roughness heatmaps, and driver health scores stream to safety managers via cellular link.
