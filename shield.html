S.H.I.E.L.D.

Smart Health Intelligence for Ergonomic & Live Driving. An AI-driven safety, health, and speed intelligence ecosystem developed under the Bharat Safety & Mobility Innovation Program and incubated at AIC IIT Delhi. Engineered to protect two-wheeler riders and commercial drivers, S.H.I.E.L.D. mitigates road vibration exposure, guides real-time safe speeds, and prevents long-term occupational health deterioration.

ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Harmful Road Vibrations Lead to Occupational Risks

India has one of the highest populations of commercial drivers and two-wheeler users globally. Millions of drivers are exposed daily to poor road infrastructure, long driving hours, fatigue, and improper posture.

The Problem

  • Chronic Back Pain: Repetitive whole-body vibrations cause spinal compression and long-term disk wear.
  • Driver Fatigue: Continuous micro-vibrations accelerate drowsiness, lowering driver alertness.
  • Unsafe Speeding: Drivers speed through high-vibration pothole zones without real-time road severity visibility.
  • Accident Hazards: Physical stress accumulation leads to slower driver reaction times and higher safety risks.

S.H.I.E.L.D. Way

  • Real-Time Guidance: High-precision vibration sensors continuously analyze shocks to suggest safer driving speeds.
  • Personalized Coaching: Provides live health advice, rest breaks, and posture-correction cues based on driver fatigue.
  • Affordable Tech: Rugged, cost-effective edge hardware built specifically for Indian road infrastructure.
  • Fleet Dashboard Licensing: Central portal for operators to monitor real-time driver comfort index and vehicle wear-and-tear.

AI-Driven Intelligence

AI-Driven Health & Speed Advisory

High-Precision Vibration Sensor

Rugged accelerometer modules continuously measure 3-axis road-induced shock, mapping bump amplitude and surface anomalies in real time.

IoT Edge-to-Cloud Pipeline

Raw vibration profiles are processed locally on the edge node and synced to the cloud dashboard, calculating the spinal comfort index and fatigue load.

Dynamic Speed & Health Advice

The on-device intelligence recommends an optimal safe speed for the current road condition, followed by active alerts to stretch, change posture, or take rest breaks.
