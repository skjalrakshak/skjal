S.H.I.E.L.D.

Smart Health Intelligence for Ergonomic & Live Driving — an AI-driven system that continuously monitors vibration exposure and provides real-time personalized safety guidance for drivers across India.

Back to Home

The Challenge

Why S.H.I.E.L.D. Exists

India has one of the highest populations of commercial drivers and two-wheeler users globally. Millions are exposed daily to hazardous conditions that threaten their health and safety.

The Problem

  • Poor road infrastructure causing harmful vibrations
  • Long driving hours leading to chronic fatigue
  • Improper posture causing chronic back pain
  • Unsafe speeding with no real-time guidance
  • Health deterioration over months and years

Our Solution

  • Vibration sensor monitors real-time exposure levels
  • AI calculates safe speed based on road conditions
  • Personalized health advice delivered in real time
  • Affordable tech accessible to every driver
  • Fleet-wide dashboards for operator visibility

Core Technology

How S.H.I.E.L.D. Works

Vibration Sensing

High-precision accelerometers detect whole-body vibration patterns in real time — mapping road conditions to health risk levels instantly.

AI Speed Intelligence

Machine learning algorithms process sensor data to calculate the optimal safe speed — delivering personalized guidance to the driver in real time.

Health Coaching

Real-time health advice based on cumulative vibration exposure — coaching drivers to reduce risk of chronic back pain, fatigue, and injury.
