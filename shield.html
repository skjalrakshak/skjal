Driver Monitoring & Fleet Safety: Ergonomic Telemetry for Commercial Vehicles.

Developed by SK Jalrakshak Innovations Private Limited, S.H.I.E.L.D. (Smart Health Intelligence for Ergonomic & Live Driving) is an advanced vehicle safety and driver health telemetry platform engineered for commercial fleets in India. By measuring continuous whole-body vibration (WBV) exposure according to ISO 2631-1 standards, road shock events, and driving kinematics, S.H.I.E.L.D. provides in-cabin alerts and fleet management analytics to assist fleet safety managers in monitoring mechanical road shock and occupational cabin ergonomics.

Protection grounded in biometric & kinematic physics.

Traditional telematics tracks only GPS coordinates. S.H.I.E.L.D. tracks the driver's physical well-being, road shocks, and cognitive alertness in real time.

Whole-Body Vibration (WBV)

Triaxial MEMS accelerometers measure continuous vehicle vibration acceleration in accordance with ISO 2631-1 guidelines, calculating Daily Exposure A(8) and Vibration Dose Values (VDV) to monitor road shock.

  • Evaluation StandardISO 2631-1 Guidelines
  • Measurement AxisTriaxial (X, Y, Z) Dynamic
  • Frequency Range0.5 Hz – 100 Hz Filtered
  • Threshold AlertsExposure Action Value (EAV)
  • Mounting OptionsChassis & Cabin Floor Mount

Driving Duration & Cabin Telemetry

Continuous trip tracking monitors cumulative driving hours, shift duration, cabin temperature, and severe shock events to alert drivers when scheduled rest intervals are recommended.

  • Duration MonitoringContinuous Shift Tracking
  • Motion AnalyticsHarsh Braking & Swerve Alerts
  • Audible WarningsIn-Cabin Audio Buzzer / Voice
  • EnvironmentalCabin Temperature & Humidity
  • Operator LoggingTrip Session & Event Timestamps

Vehicle Bus & Speed Context

Direct CAN-bus integration reads engine RPM, harsh braking, aggressive lane maneuvers, combined with high-precision GNSS position and terrain slope.

  • Vehicle BusOBD-II, J1939 & CAN 2.0B
  • PositioningMulti-GNSS (GPS, GLONASS, NavIC)
  • Incident Log100Hz Black-Box Pre-Crash Buffer

In-cabin edge intelligence workflow.

Low-latency edge processing ensures drivers receive instant audible coaching before an accident occurs, not hours later in a back-office report.

STEP 01

Seat & Cabin Sensing

Vibration sensors on the seat base and cabin sensors stream high-frequency kinematic and ambient data to the edge unit.

STEP 02

Edge Ergonomic Engine

The on-board processor computes vibration dose values (VDV) and ergonomic exposure indicators locally in real time.

STEP 03

Immediate Driver Audio

If continuous driving duration or cumulative vibration thresholds are breached, the unit delivers clear audible prompts advising a rest break.

STEP 04

Fleet Analytics Cloud

Aggregated risk profiles, route roughness heatmaps, and driver health scores stream to safety managers via cellular link.

Frequently Asked Questions

Technical answers regarding driver vibration monitoring, ISO 2631 compliance, fleet portal capabilities, and vehicle hardware integration.

1. What does the S.H.I.E.L.D. driver safety system monitor?

S.H.I.E.L.D. monitors continuous whole-body vibration (WBV) according to ISO 2631-1 and ISO 5349 standards, road shock events, driving duration indicators, cabin environmental conditions (temperature, CO2, VOCs), and driving kinematics via CAN bus and high-precision multi-GNSS positioning.

2. Which vehicles and fleets can use S.H.I.E.L.D.?

The platform is designed for commercial heavy transport fleets, mining and quarry dumpers, construction equipment, intercity passenger buses, logistics delivery vehicles, and utility fleets operating on demanding highways and rough terrain.

3. What information can fleet operators access?

Fleet supervisors access a cloud-based dashboard displaying real-time vibration dose values (VDV), cumulative daily exposure A(8), harsh braking and aggressive lane change alerts, continuous driving duration tracking, speed compliance, and trip replay with pre-crash black-box incident logs.

4. How is the device integrated into a commercial vehicle?

S.H.I.E.L.D. installs non-invasively without altering vehicle warranty. Hardware comprises a thin piezo-resistive sensor mat positioned on the driver's seat base, an in-cabin edge controller with audible voice guidance, an optional OBD-II/J1939 CAN harness, and an encrypted 4G cellular uplink.
