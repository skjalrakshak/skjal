Fleet & Rider Safety

S.H.I.E.L.D. is a revolutionary safety intelligence ecosystem. By utilizing predictive spatial tracking and deep AI analytics, the system actively monitors driver fatigue, distraction, and vehicle dynamics.

Whole-Body Vibration Tracking

Continuously tracks Whole-Body Vibration (WBV) exposure and road shock. Alerts drivers to extreme conditions to actively prevent long-term occupational health deterioration and spinal issues.

Real-Time Fatigue Alerts

Leverages live biometric indicators and driving pattern anomalies to detect driver fatigue in real time, delivering immediate, personalized safety protocols and warnings to prevent collisions.

Intelligence Ecosystem

How S.H.I.E.L.D. prevents occupational hazards and collisions in real-time.

The Edge

MEMS & Biometric Sensors

Advanced 3-Axis MEMS accelerometers track high-frequency road shocks and Whole-Body Vibrations (WBV), while cabin sensors monitor driver posture and fatigue indicators.

Telemetry

OBD-II & GPS Integration

Spatial dynamics are correlated with vehicle telemetry (OBD-II data) and transmitted instantly via 4G/LTE, guaranteeing zero latency during critical safety events.

Analytics

ISO 2631 Compliance AI

The system analyzes vibrations against global health standards (ISO 2631) to predict lumbar spinal damage, dispatching localized haptic alerts to the driver and reports to the fleet manager.

Target Applications

Commercial Logistics

Protecting long-haul truck drivers from chronic spinal injuries caused by continuous road vibrations.

Two-Wheeler Delivery Fleets

Real-time fatigue and posture monitoring for last-mile delivery riders exposed to constant urban shocks.

Mining & Construction

Tracking extreme multi-axis vibration exposure for operators of heavy excavation and earthmoving machinery.
