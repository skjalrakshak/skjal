AI-Driven Fleet & Rider Safety

S.H.I.E.L.D. is a revolutionary safety intelligence ecosystem engineered for commercial fleets and two-wheeler riders. By utilizing predictive spatial tracking and deep AI analytics, the system actively monitors driver fatigue, distraction, and vehicle dynamics. It mitigates road vibration exposure, guides real-time safe speeds, and actively prevents occupational health deterioration.

AI Dashcam Vision

Our advanced optical lenses use predictive spatial tracking to actively monitor driver fatigue, distraction, and unsafe driving behaviors in real-time.

Ergonomic Health Tracking

Monitors prolonged vibrations and poor driving postures, alerting drivers to prevent long-term occupational health deterioration and spinal issues.

An ultra-premium dashboard for logistics managers to predict maintenance, optimize routes, and enforce safety protocols across thousands of vehicles.
